mysurflife.

Two concepts, six variations each, plus contextual usage. Swell-ring mark (echoes the map's swell-generation rings) and M-as-wave monogram. Bold & athletic mood, optimized for horizontal header + hero use.

The thinking: the app's core metaphor is a storm, somewhere, sending waves to your spot. The mark should carry that. Pure wordmarks feel weak for a surf brand — we commit to a symbol.
